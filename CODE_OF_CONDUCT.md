# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a project about software that manages people's employment
records — treat the people in the project at least as well.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are two people with different context.
- Critique the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time, and that "not now" and "not this"
  are legitimate answers.
- Respect that contributors work in many countries, timezones and first
  languages. Plain English beats clever English.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption,
publishing someone's private information, or unwelcome attention of any kind.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/nazmul284). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that breach this document,
and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
